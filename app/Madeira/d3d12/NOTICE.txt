This folder holds what the app needs from Apple's Metal Shader Converter.

- libmetalirconverter.dylib (iOS build): Apple proprietary. NOT in the source
  repository. Distributed inside built Madeira packages under the Apple
  Metal Shader Converter agreement, section 2.B ("install, use, test, and
  distribute the Dynamic Libraries for the sole purpose of shader
  conversion"); Madeira uses it solely to convert DXIL shaders to Metal.
  Full agreement: METAL-SHADER-CONVERTER-AGREEMENT.txt. Obtain it with
  build/madeira-d3d12/fetch-converter.sh from Apple's installer package.
- Metal Shader Converter headers (metal_irconverter*, compiled into the
  runtime): Apache License 2.0, Copyright Apple Inc.
  See LICENSE-metal-shader-converter-headers.txt.
- canary_*.dxil: Madeira's own test shaders (GPL-3.0-or-later with the
  draft additional permission in the repository's LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md).
- LICENSE-MADEIRA-GPL-3.0.txt and LICENSE-MADEIRA-EXCEPTION.txt: copies of
  the repository's COPYING and LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md, staged into this
  bundled folder by build/madeira-d3d12/fetch-converter.sh so the shipped
  app carries the licence and the (draft) additional permission. They are
  generated copies and are not tracked in git.
